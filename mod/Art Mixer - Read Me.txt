ART MIXER
=====================

Install several art mods at once and choose, in the MCM, how much of each one you see. Install three
painting mods and set them to 50, 30 and 20: half the paintings show the first mod's art, 30% the second's
and 20% the third's. Set them lower and some paintings stay vanilla.

Covers paintings, posters, signs, magazines, quest pictures, props and set dressing, the Pip-Boy screen and
skin, the Pip-Boy world map, magazine covers on loading screens, and the main menu background and music.

Works with every version of Fallout 4 from 1.10.163 to current with the same files (untested on VR),
under Mod Organizer 2 or Vortex.


REQUIREMENTS
------------
  - F4SE for your game version (f4se.silverlock.org)
  - Mod Configuration Menu 1.39 or later (Nexus 21497)
  - Runtime Database (Nexus 108394)
  - .NET 10 Desktop Runtime, x64 (dotnet.microsoft.com/download/dotnet/10.0), for the converter only


INSTALL
-------
1. Install the requirements.
2. Install Art Mixer with your mod manager and enable ArtMixer.esl.
3. Install your art mods as usual, with whatever installer options you like.
4. Run the converter (see CONVERTER), press "Find installed art mods", then "Convert".
5. Enable the new "Art Mixer Packs" mod:
     Mod Organizer 2: press F5, enable it and place it BELOW Art Mixer.
     Vortex: install "Art Mixer Packs.zip" from Downloads, enable it, let it win over
     Art Mixer.
   You can now disable the art mods you converted. Keep a mod enabled if it also adds new objects of its
   own; Art Mixer still decides what its replaced art shows.
6. In game, open MCM > Art Mixer, set the sliders and press "Apply all".


CONVERTER
---------
The converter is Tools\ArtMixer\Art Mixer.exe inside the mod. Add it as a tool:
  Mod Organizer 2: Executables (gears icon) > Add > browse to
    <instance>\mods\Art Mixer\Tools\ArtMixer\Art Mixer.exe
  Vortex: Dashboard > Add tool > browse to Data\Tools\ArtMixer\Art Mixer.exe

It finds your Mod Organizer 2 instances and Vortex by itself. "Find installed art mods" lists the mods that
hold art, grouped by the kind of art each holds most of; enabled ones are ticked. The "Props and set
dressing" group starts collapsed: open it to see which of those mods will be converted. Mods in the
"Mostly not art" group (graphics overhauls, item sorters and other mods that mostly change things other
than art) start collapsed and unticked: tick one to convert its art. Each ticked mod becomes one pack named after the mod (rename the mod in
your manager to rename the pack). Every conversion rebuilds all packs from the ticked mods. The log and
ArtMixer\convert-report.txt in the output say what was taken from each mod and what was left to it.

Run it again whenever you add, remove or change art mods. Converting the same mods keeps your settings; a
different set of mods resets every setting to its default.

Only art is converted. Anything else a mod changes (bodies, armor, weapons, interface, sounds, map
interface mods) stays with that mod, so keep such a mod enabled.

Without a mod manager: unpack each art mod into its own folder and run
  "Art Mixer.exe" --game "<Fallout 4 folder>" --out "<empty folder>" <art mod folder> ...
then copy the contents of the output folder into Fallout 4\Data, replacing files.


MENU
----
  Paintings, Posters, Signage, Magazines, Quest
                   One slider per pack whose art is all of that one kind: the percent of everything that
                   pack replaces that shows its art. Sliders never move each other. When packs replace the
                   same object, their percents share it; the rest is vanilla.
  Mixed packs      One slider per pack with art of more than one kind, such as magazine covers and
                   paintings, or props and signs. It sets all of that pack's art.
  Other replacers  One on/off switch per pack, for props and set dressing.
  Pip-Boy          Pick the screen background and the skin: Vanilla or one pack each.
  World map        Pick the Pip-Boy world map: Vanilla or one pack. A map drawn at a different size keeps
                   its markers lined up.
  Menus            Two sections.
                   Main menu: Themes, Backgrounds and Music, the same picks as the "MENU ART" entry on
                   the main menu. With Random Main Menu (Nexus 74187) installed, a Random Main Menu box
                   appears below them. Off: your picks play. On: your packs join its draw, by "Rotate
                   themes" (a background with its music), "Rotate backgrounds" (the Music pick stays) or
                   "Rotate music" (the Backgrounds pick stays). With Immersive Main Menus (Nexus 82077)
                   installed as well, its box adds its menus to the lists and to the draw, and
                   "Immersive Main Menus only" keeps your picks out of the draw. Neither mod is
                   required; the boxes appear only when the mod is found at game start.
                   Loading screens: one slider per pack with magazine covers, the percent of loading
                   screens that show its covers. With Automated Loading Screen Replacer (Nexus 52322)
                   installed, a switch of that name appears above them: off, Art Mixer runs every
                   loading screen and Automated Loading Screen Replacer does nothing; on, the loading
                   screens are its and the sliders go away.

  Apply all        Applies your changes. The game saves as ArtMixerApply and reloads it, so everything updates
                   at once. Leaving the menu with changes not applied asks "Apply all" or "Cancel".
  Shuffle layout   Same percents, new arrangement.
  New layout on every load   Off by default.
  Reset all settings         Everything back to vanilla.


UNINSTALL
---------
Disable Art Mixer and Art Mixer Packs. Items you picked up while they showed a
pack's name keep that name; everything else returns to vanilla.


KNOWN ISSUES
------------
  - A pack's slider is the share of the art that pack ships, not of every object of that kind. A
    magazine pack that covers 59 of the game's 135 magazine covers set to 50 changes about a quarter
    of all magazines.
  - The first seconds after a load can stutter while the game's own textures are read from its
    archives for Vanilla picks, more with the High Resolution Texture Pack installed.
  - Apply all saves the game as ArtMixerApply and reloads it; a save made at that moment is part of the
    design.
  - Converting a different set of mods resets every setting, and the game reloads once at the next
    start to show the reset ("packs changed, settings reset; reloading").
  - Untested on Fallout 4 VR.


TROUBLESHOOTING
---------------
  - Random Main Menu plays something other than your pick: its box on the Menus page is on with a
    Rotate switch, which is the draw working as set. With the box off, the log carries
    "Random Main Menu's menu calls hooked"; a line ending "stay in control of the menu" means the
    plugin could not take the menu over on your game version, so send the log.
  - Automated Loading Screen Replacer shows nothing: its switch on the Menus page is off, which is the
    default; turn it on to hand the loading screens to it.
  - Files missing or damaged: the game names them at startup. Open the converter: under Mod Organizer 2 it
    restores them; otherwise reinstall Art Mixer.
  - Log file: Documents\My Games\Fallout4\F4SE\ArtMixer.log
